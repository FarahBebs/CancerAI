This is a course project for comparing image classifiers. A score is how strongly an image matches a training folder. It is not a diagnosis, not a risk estimate, and not something to use for a health decision. A tumor is not the same thing as cancer. Brain labels do not say benign or malignant. Actinic keratoses are precancerous and are grouped here as cancer-related only because that is the dataset convention. The models are not validated for clinical use.
